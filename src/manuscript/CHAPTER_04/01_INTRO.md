In this chapter we explore the radical generosity at the heart of Jesus’ teaching. Across the spreads, we move from the world’s logic of visible giving, impressive sums, and strategic hospitality, to the true call: give quietly, give sacrificially, and give to those who cannot repay. 

Each spread invites you to examine your motives, shift your focus from recognition to mercy, and practice generosity that transforms both giver and recipient. Reflection prompts and simple practices will help you embody a generosity that is hidden, costly, and open-handed, mirroring the way of Christ, and forming your mind and heart in alignment with His.

Consider how Jesus redefines generosity not as a means to gain status or favor, but as an expression of love and grace. Let these teachings inspire you to cultivate a heart that gives freely, without expectation, and embraces those in need with compassion and humility. As with all of Jesus’ teachings, there is a countercultural element here and there is no profit in it for the giver aside from their own transformation.

Tribalism is the way of things in the modern world just as it was in the ancient world. People naturally gravitate toward groups of people who seem like them in some way, forming tight-knit groups based on shared characteristics, beliefs, or interests and a sense of belonging and community, but it can also create divisions and barriers between different groups. Consider the many churches who all claim the same goals, and yet are enemies of one another still.

In the context of generosity, tribalism can influence who we choose to help and how we perceive outsiders to our circles. Jesus' teachings challenge us to transcend that kind of boundary, and encourage us to extend our generosity beyond.

The difference, at the core, is asking who can be called your neighbor. We are no longer wandering only on foot and limited to those in close proximity; instead, we can connect with and assist people from all walks of life, regardless of geographical or social boundaries. This expanded understanding of neighborliness calls us to a higher standard of generosity -- one that reflects the love and mercy that Jesus exemplified.
