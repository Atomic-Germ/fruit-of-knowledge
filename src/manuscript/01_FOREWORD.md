\vspace*{1in}

I see **deeply confused** Christians in churches Struggling to see the Truth in a darkened mirror whose pieces are split into disconnected passages and filtered through the understandings, opinions, views, bias, and ambitions, of teachers, preachers, marketers and leaders. These are the hipocrites Jesus warned of so much, or at best the blinded leaders also spoken of.  

The problem -- arguably the only _real_ problem -- is inherent to human beings on the whole. People have no idea as to what that symbolic fruit that started all of this chaos even represented. The **Fruit of the Knowledge of Good and Evil**; that is to say **the result of knowing right from wrong**; that is, the conscience or even the very nature of The Spirit we are given. The fruit is always the result of a thing, and the conscience results from the knowledge of good and evil. 

The pople who seek it are lead in comforting scriptural circles forever in exchange for the weekly donation; or else be caught up in some angry ferver or other toward the marginalized target of the week. They have no idea what it is, or what it does, or how to get it, and often even why they even might want to. They have no idea that it is the very thing that Jesus came to give us, and that it is the very thing that the enemy has been trying to take from us since the beginning. **We** were always the enemy. They cannot find it; but thank God that they can't, because the truth of it would burn terribly with guilt fo so many unchangeable pasts. God help them.

This part of us that knows more certainly than the world can teach or bend us to; this is the _direct_ knowledge of what is good and what is evil, and how to discern between the two. It is the reason that the first people had such intense shame before the God they realized had always watched and always listened to them. It is the reason that they hid from Him, and covered themselves with the leaves from that same tree; which was a fig tree[^00].It is the reason that they were "cast out" of paradise; it was the paradise of innocence. The bliss of ignorance as to right and wrong, and accountability.  

At some point, we have to grow up, and to stand in the sight of the one who judges us all. We have to take responsibility for our actions, and the consequences of those actions. We have to confess to already knowing how to discern between what is good and what is evil, and that we each have our own agency with which to do either good or evil at will.  

If it is Jesus of Nazareth you say you follow, this was his gift to us. This message. This **is the knowledge of good and evil** which you already have, and may have forgotten. If both Jesus and Satan agree on what is good and what is evil, then you can be sure that it is so. You can be as you are, and know truly who that is.

---

[^00]:Gen 3:7 --- _The fig tree is significant because it is the only tree mentioned by name in the entire Genesis creation account. It is also the tree that Jesus cursed in the New Testament, symbolizing the judgment_. "And the eyes of them both were opened, and they knew that they were naked"
